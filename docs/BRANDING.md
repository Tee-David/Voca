# Voca Brand Notes

## Name

**Voca**

Short, voice-adjacent and broad enough for a reading platform rather than a single text-to-speech utility.

## Tagline

**Read anything. Listen anywhere.**

## Core statement

Voca is an open-source, local-first reader that turns your documents into a synchronized listening experience.

## Product belief

People should not have to repurchase a document as an audiobook, maintain another subscription or automatically upload private reading material to a proprietary cloud service just to listen to something they already own.

## Promise

**Your books. Your device. Your reading experience.**

## Voice

Voca should sound technically credible, calm and direct. Explain what is local and what is remote. Avoid pretending optional AI is the product's foundation. Avoid exaggerated claims about privacy, offline behavior, performance or speech quality that the current release cannot demonstrate.

## Open-source presentation

The repository should communicate the problem before the stack. Architecture diagrams should make the local reading path obvious. Screenshots and demos should show real product behavior using copyright-safe material. Security and privacy documentation should be discoverable rather than hidden at the bottom of the repository.
