The maintainer is responsible for the project's technical direction, release approval, security coordination and final decisions when contributor consensus cannot be reached.

## Decision principles

Maintenance decisions should prioritize:

1. a strong reading and listening experience;
2. local-first capability and understandable privacy boundaries;
3. security of document and account data;
4. provider independence;
5. accessibility;
6. maintainable, testable engineering;
7. a contributor experience that does not depend on private infrastructure.

## Contributions and ownership

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions while licensing accepted contributions under the project's eventual source license. The exact source license will be documented before the first public release.

The project may add additional maintainers as the contributor community develops. Maintainer access should follow demonstrated trust and project involvement rather than being granted solely to reduce workload.
