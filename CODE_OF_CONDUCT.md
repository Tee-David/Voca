# Code of Conduct

Voca should be a place where people can build, learn, review and disagree constructively.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Assume good intent where reasonable, but take security, privacy and accessibility concerns seriously. Give contributors enough context to act on feedback. Welcome people with different levels of experience and avoid gatekeeping.

Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, private documents or security-sensitive data. Do not use project spaces for harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, spam or deliberate disruption.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is normal. Prefer reproducible evidence, tests, benchmarks, documented tradeoffs and small experiments over status or rhetoric. Maintainers may make a final project decision after relevant perspectives have been heard.

## Security discussions

Potential vulnerabilities should follow SECURITY.md rather than being disclosed publicly before they can be assessed. This is not a request to hide ordinary criticism or known public information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or take other proportionate action when behavior materially harms contributors or the project. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from project-managed spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the maintainer using the contact path documented in SECURITY.md. Reports will be reviewed with attention to both the reported behavior and the surrounding context.

## Scope

This code applies in Voca-managed repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when someone is officially representing the project.
